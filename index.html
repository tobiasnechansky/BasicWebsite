My Basic Website

Recreate this website using only what we learnt in units 1 to 3: a clean HTML structure and CSS rules built from element selectors, combinators and pseudo-classes. The whole header – including the logo – is centered.

Text and Fonts

This first paragraph after a level two heading is written in italics – just like the first paragraph after every other level two heading.

The whole page uses a font without serifs, darkslategray as text color and ivory as background color. The line height is 1.5.

Headings of level one and two are navy. Level two headings are centered and written in uppercase letters – but not in the HTML source,changing the look of text is the job of CSS!

Some words are strongly important: they are bold – as usual – and have a yellow background. Others are only emphasized and keep the default style of the browser.

Links

Links like this one to the MDN CSS reference are darkgreen and not underlined – even after they have been visited. When the mouse pointer hovers over a link, it is underlined and gets a lightgreen background

Lists

All three kinds of lists appear on this page. Every bullet point and every number is crimson.
